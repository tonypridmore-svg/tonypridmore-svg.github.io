Macro Intelligence

Macro Intelligence is a private Windows desktop research application. It collects public macroeconomic news and official economic data, estimates how markets may react before the Asia, London and New York trading sessions, emails its owner a plain-English report, and later checks how accurate each estimate was.

What it does

  • Reads public news feeds and official statistics (for example from central banks and government statistics offices).
  • Produces Bullish / Bearish / Neutral probabilities for two futures markets: Micro E-mini Nasdaq-100 (MNQ) and Micro Gold (MGC).
  • Sends the report by email from the owner's own Gmail account to an address the owner chooses.
  • Scores past forecasts against what actually happened, to test whether the method is useful.

It is a research tool only. It does not trade, does not connect to any broker or trading account, and does not give financial advice.

Who can use it

Macro Intelligence is a personal project used by its owner on their own computer. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up and no online service, and no account is needed to read this page.

How it uses Google

The app asks Google for one permission only: to send email on the owner's behalf (the gmail.send permission). It cannot read, search, change or delete any email, contact or other Google data. Details are in the privacy policy.
