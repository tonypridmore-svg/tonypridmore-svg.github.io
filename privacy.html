Privacy policy

Macro Intelligence · Last updated 18 October 2026

Macro Intelligence is a private Windows desktop application used by its owner on their own computer. This policy explains what Google user data the app can access and what it does with it.

Google data the app can access

The app requests a single Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send, which lets it send email from the owner's Gmail account. It does not request, and cannot use, permission to read, search, label, change or delete email, or to access contacts, calendar, files or any other Google data.

How that data is used

  • The permission is used only to send the app's own forecast reports, alerts and test messages to an email address chosen by the owner in the app's settings.
  • No Google user data is sold, rented, shared with or transferred to anyone.
  • No Google user data is used for advertising, for building user profiles, or for training any artificial intelligence (AI) model.

Where data is stored

  • The Google permission token is stored only on the owner's computer, in Windows Credential Manager (the secure password store built into Windows). It is never written to log files.
  • Copies of sent reports and a delivery log are kept in a local database on the same computer, so the owner can review past reports.
  • There is no server or online service run for this app. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Other services the app uses

To write its forecasts, the app sends public news headlines and public economic data to Anthropic's Claude, through the owner's own Claude account. This never includes Google user data. The app also downloads public news feeds and public statistics from their publishers.

Keeping and deleting data

  • The owner can remove the Google permission at any time with Disconnect in the app, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app offers to delete its database, logs and backups from the computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Macro Intelligence's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
